Audience: lead dev. Goal: fold the new components from the mockup into the existing High & Low site (believed to be plain HTML/JS; confirm), and use the NJ platform scan to decide which menu scrapers to build next.

Status of everything here: design mockup (static, no behavior), scan data (real, October 2026), spec (this doc, a proposal). Items marked **[NEEDS REVIEW]** are legal or business decisions, not engineering ones.

## 2. What changes on the site

Four additions to the existing results and product pages. Nothing in the existing price-comparison logic needs to change.

1. **Gear strip** (contextual affiliate row) on results and product pages.
2. **Single Sponsored slot** on results.
3. **Footer**: 21+ notice and affiliate disclosure.
4. **Product detail page** layout: price trio (lowest / highest / you save), best-deal panel, comparison table, related products.

Also in the mockup, already existing in concept: result card with price spread, store rows with a "Best deal" variant, phone "Show N more stores" collapse.

## 4. Components

### 4.1 Result card
Shows one product with its price spread across stores.
- Inputs: `product` (name, image, size/unit), `offers[]` (store, price, distance, in_stock).
- Derived: `lowest`, `highest`, `spread = highest - lowest`.
- Store rows sorted by price ascending. First row gets the "Best deal" style.
- Phone: show the best 3 rows, "Show N more stores" expands the rest.

### 4.2 Gear strip (affiliate)
- Placed after the 3rd result card on results, and below the comparison table on detail (4 tiles).
- Contextual: the tile set depends on product category. Proposed map: flower -> grinders, jars, rolling trays; vape -> batteries, cases; edibles -> storage, scales; concentrates -> dab tools, mats. Keep it config-driven (see section 7).
- Each tile: image, title, price (if known), button. Link must carry `rel="sponsored noopener"` and `target="_blank"`.
- Label the strip clearly ("Gear, affiliate links"). Do not mimic a dispensary result.

### 4.3 Sponsored slot
One slot only, clearly labeled "Sponsored", visually distinct from organic results, never ranked into the price ordering. Intended for a direct vendor ad sale later; ship empty or house-filled.

### 4.4 Footer
21+ notice and affiliate disclosure copy (see mockup). Disclosure must also appear near the first affiliate placement on each page, not only in the footer. **[NEEDS REVIEW]** FTC wording.

### 4.5 Product detail
Breadcrumb; hero (image, name, size); trio (Lowest, Highest, You save); Best-deal panel (store, price, address, "Get directions" and "View menu" buttons); comparison table (store, price, brand/THC placeholders, distance, last updated); gear strip; related products.

Mockup caveat: **brand and THC columns are placeholders.** The scan does not provide them and they require scraper work (section 6).

## 5. Behavior the mockup does not show

- Search, sort, filter (distance, in-stock only, category), pagination or infinite scroll.
- Loading, empty and error states; stale-data indicator ("updated 3h ago"). Stale prices are the biggest trust risk.
- Store "View menu" deep link: send the user to the store's own menu page. Vendor URL patterns are in the scan JSON.
- 21+ age gate on first visit (cookie/localStorage). **[NEEDS REVIEW]** whether a gate is required for a price-comparison site; most cannabis sites have one and it is cheap insurance.
- Analytics events: result impression, store row click, gear click, sponsored click.

## 6. Data contract

### 6.1 What the scan gives you (real)
Per store, in `data/nj_dispensary_platforms.json` and the xlsx: name, town, county, zip, address, lat/long, phone, website, detected menu platform, confidence, the vendor hosts seen, and `api_paths` (data calls the menu makes: starting point for each scraper).

### 6.2 What a price scraper must add (not in the scan)
```
offer = {
  store_id, product_id (normalized), raw_name,
  brand, category, strain_type, thc_pct, size, unit,
  price, sale_price, in_stock, scraped_at, source_url
}
```
Needs a normalization layer to match the same product across stores (brand + name + size). That matching is likely the hardest part of the product and is separate from scraping.

## 7. Affiliate configuration

Keep affiliate data out of markup. Suggested `affiliates.json`:
```
{ "categories": { "flower": [ {"id":"grinder-x","title":"...","img":"...","price":"$24",
   "url":"https://network/deep-link","program":"awin","merchant":"..."} ] },
  "disclosure": "..." }
```
- Route every outbound gear click through your own `/go/<id>` endpoint (redirect + log): gives click counts, lets you swap links without redeploying pages, and survives program changes. Plain HTML/JS can do this with a small serverless function or a static redirect table.
- Log: timestamp, tile id, page, category, anonymized session. No PII.
- Cookies: networks attribute on last click within a window (commonly 30 days). Links do not "expire" on their own, but programs get paused, merchants leave a network, and deep links break, so add a monthly link check. See `business-notes.md`.

## 8. Compliance and risk **[NEEDS REVIEW]**

1. NJ cannabis advertising rules (CRC) may apply to sponsored placements and to anything that looks like dispensary promotion. Get counsel's read before selling a Sponsored slot to a licensee.
2. Paraphernalia and accessory affiliate programs have their own terms; many networks restrict cannabis-adjacent merchants. Confirm each program accepts the site.
3. FTC affiliate disclosure placement and wording.
4. Scraping: detecting a menu vendor is not the same as having the right to pull prices. Review each vendor's terms (Dutchie, Dispense, Sweed first) and each store's. Prefer data partnerships where possible. The scanner itself only loads public pages at a low rate.
5. Age-restricted audience: no ad networks that target minors; no cannabis ads via Google/Meta display.

## 9. Integrating into the existing site

Unknown stack (believed plain HTML/JS). Suggested approach:

3. Replace placeholder copy, images and affiliate links with data from `affiliates.json`.
